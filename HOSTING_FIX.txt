RPC HOSTING FIX

This package includes: 
1. Cache-busted style.css and script.js references.
2. A robust leadership grid width fix for the About page.
3. All original website files and ministry photos.

IMPORTANT cPanel upload:
- Extract the ZIP on your computer.
- Upload/overwrite ALL files in public_html (or the document root), including style.css and script.js.
- Keep the assets/ folder and its subfolders intact.
- Do not upload the ZIP itself as the live site.
- After uploading, use Ctrl+F5 in Chrome.
- If your hosting has a cache option, purge/clear it.
