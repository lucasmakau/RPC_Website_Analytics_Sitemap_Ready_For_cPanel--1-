The Welfare Ministry reflects the church’s commitment to practical care and support for people in need, strengthening the church family and reaching the wider community with compassion.

Serving People

The ministry supports the church’s wider commitment to the welfare of people and to practical care for those who may need encouragement and support.

Community Care

Through the church’s social initiatives, the ministry contributes to a caring church family where people can find fellowship, prayer and practical support.

Where We Are Going

We seek to strengthen support for vulnerable individuals and families through organised care, practical assistance and compassionate follow-up, helping the church respond to needs with dignity and consistency.
