RPC Journey

Our journey so far.

Every season has added something to the story of Rock Priesthood Church. What began with a call to serve has grown into a church family with a continuing vision.

The Story

Faith, people and growth.

Our journey is more than the buildings we have put up. It is the story of people responding to God's call, serving one another and trusting Him through every season.

The Beginning

A call to serve

Rev. Patrick Musa answered the call to serve the Lord and began preparing for ministry. Through training and service in different ministries, the calling grew into a vision that would become part of the foundation of Rock Priesthood Church.

Pioneer Years

Rock Priesthood Church takes shape

Rev. Patrick Musa pioneered Rock Priesthood Church alongside Pst Purity and an enabled team of committed people who shared the vision and helped establish the church family. Together, they began building a community centred on worship, the Word of God, fellowship and the vision of raising a Godly and royal generation.

The Mikuyu Project

A place of worship begins to take shape

At Mikuyu, Mikuyu, Vota, Machakos, Kenya, the work has grown one step at a time. The land was cleared, the boundary was established, the gate was developed and church facilities began to take shape. Each stage has brought the vision closer to a place where people can gather, worship and fellowship.

Today & Beyond

This far, the Lord has been Ebenezer.

Today, the journey continues. RPC remains committed to worship, community, service and the growth of the church at Mikuyu. There is still more to do, but there is also much to be grateful for. With faith and gratitude, we look ahead to what God will do next.

The journey continues.

From the first steps to the work happening today, Rock Priesthood Church looks ahead with faith and gratitude.
