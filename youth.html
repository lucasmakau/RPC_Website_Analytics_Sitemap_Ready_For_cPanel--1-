Youth Ministry

Faith. Leadership. Impact.

“Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.” — 1 Timothy 4:12

Our Youth Ministry is a dynamic space dedicated to raising young people who lead by example, stand firm in their faith and walk in purity and purpose. We are not just waiting for the future—we are building strategic leaders for today.

Life & Service

Growing together.

Where We Are Today

We gather for authentic worship, engaging life-groups and creative arts that address real-world questions and foster deep, Christ-centered community.

Building Responsible Leaders & Priests

We are intentionally discipling young adults and teens to become responsible leaders and spiritual priests in their generation—empowering them to honour God, take ownership of their calling and leave a lasting impact.

Where We Are Going

We are launching dedicated leadership training tracks, expanding digital media ministry and introducing career-readiness workshops to prepare our youth for transformational influence in every sphere of life.
