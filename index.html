Welcome to RPC

A place to worship, grow and serve.

Rock Priesthood Church exists to provide a place for worship of the Lord God, Christian fellowship and the sharing of the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Our calling is rooted in raising a Godly and royal generation and nurturing people who know God, live by His Word and declare His praises.
